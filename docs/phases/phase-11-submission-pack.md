# Phase 11. Submission pack

## Goal

Prepare everything the hackathon submission form asks for.

## Start when

- Phase 10 is done.

## What to prepare

1. **The access link** to the live website, and the address of the DeWEB edition.
2. **The description.** Write it from the PRD:
   - The problem (section 2).
   - The product and what it does (sections 1 and 9).
   - The target users and how to reach them (section 5).
   - The revenue model and why users would pay (section 16).
3. **The list of TapeOut features used:**
   - Site records read from X Layer.
   - TapeKit, for reading and verifying files.
   - DeWEB, for hosting the on-chain edition.
4. **The demo video,** 3 minutes or less. Show, in this order:
   - The problem in one or two sentences.
   - Pasting a real site address and getting a result.
   - A planted RED test page being caught.
   - The site list with filters.
   - The badge.
   - The DeWEB edition opening through the gateway.
   - Who it is for and how it earns.
5. **Real numbers** from Phase 9: how many sites were checked and what was found.
6. **Eligibility.** Confirm it in the hackathon Telegram channel before submitting.
7. **Submit** through the form on the hackathon page.

## Done when

- Every item on the submission form is ready.
- The video is 3 minutes or less and shows the product working.
- Eligibility is confirmed.
- The form is submitted.

## Watch out

- Only work done during the event is judged. Describe what was built.
- Show the real product in the video, not slides.
- Check every link once more just before submitting.
